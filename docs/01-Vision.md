# VideoText Vision

## Mission

Convert lecture videos into structured, editable documents.

## Goals

- Recover the logical structure of presentations.
- Minimize manual editing.
- Produce high-quality exports.
- Support multiple export formats.

## Non-Goals

- Perfect OCR.
- Exact recreation of slide appearance.
- Real-time processing.

## Version 1 Success Criteria

A faculty member can process a lecture video and receive a Markdown document requiring less than 10 minutes of cleanup.
